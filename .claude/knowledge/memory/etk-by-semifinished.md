Owner agreed with Vasily (TECHMAP-GRAPHER author) on 13.10.2026: generating a techcard yields as many documents as there are semi-finished items (ПФ). The operation list is cut into ПФ groups (ПФ-1 cut, ПФ-2 crimp, … final), configured right in the ETK's left operation list (drag ops between ПФ groups, own order inside, pictures attached). Inputs/outputs between ПФ come automatically.

A fitter's task = one ПФ document: its operations (e.g. 5-6-7), quantity (e.g. 1000), quality/time requirements, the inputs taken from the previous ПФ task; the typical-operation instruction opens in detail on demand. A document may hold a single operation. Everything flows into the plan and the daily assignment.

**Why:** the flat ETK «простыня» doesn't map to how work is handed out; the ПФ is the unit of work, handover (boxes) and planning.
**How to apply:** design techcard/production changes around ПФ documents, not single ETK operations; reuse ETK blocks if they already equal ПФ. Related: [[module-techcards]], [[module-production]], [[vasily-code-permission]].
