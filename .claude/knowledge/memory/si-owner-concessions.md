18.11.2026 owner: СИ.034 (S-322, SMA розетка–SMA розетка, no maker spec) «можно считать годным до 12 ГГц», although its
assigned norm ESCC 3402/003 (VSWR 1.1+0.008·f) is exceeded slightly above ~4.8 GHz (measured ≈1.2–1.25 to 12 GHz,
resonance ~13 GHz with VSWR 1.41/1.49 and loss 1.68 dB).

Same evening the owner withdrew the concession idea: the passport must simply say «соответствует» after a proper
re-measurement — he re-connects СИ.034 on 19.11 and the verdict follows the new 3-connection measurement against its norm.
Don't build a per-item concession now. Later the owner asked (18.11 night) for a FUTURE button «закрыть глаза на небольшие
отклонения»: accept a measurement with a small deviation — role-restricted, reason required, journaled, printed as
«Допущен с отклонением …», only within a small bound over the norm. Backlog item, after the 19.11 re-measurement.

**Why:** the owner judges it fit for use; the norm verdict must stay honest, the concession is his explicit decision.
**How to apply:** keep the norm verdict as measured; record the concession per item (who/when/up to which frequency) and
print «Допущен к применению до 12 ГГц решением владельца 18.11.2026» — never fold it into the norm or invent a shop limit
([[si-applicability-limit]]). Re-measure СИ.034 first by СТО ФРАК-002 (3 connections, 120°).
