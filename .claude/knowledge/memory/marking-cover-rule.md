- Printed marks (Supvan TP80E: tape 6/9/12 мм AND printed marker tube Ø 2,4–8) have an option «покрыть прозрачной трубкой» (owner 20.11: «бывает поверх трубки и поверх ленты»). The cover length is computed
  automatically: printed label length (from the printer job/template) + 10 мм. Ø follows the wire.
- Heat-shrink tube pieces («нарезка трубок», e.g. protective ТУТ 35/55 мм) are cut on a machine, never «by hand» — bind
  the operation to that machine.
- Owner 20.11 was irritated by asking these («что за тупые вопросы»): shop-practice defaults like these are decided by
  the agent, not asked.

**Why:** owner's rule for the generator/ETK split per operation (TASKS «20.11 NEXT»).
**How to apply:** generator emits the cover piece + its cut/shrink steps when the option is on; ask only about things the
shop data can't settle. Related: [[factory-serial-and-grouped-tasks]], [[shrink-default-kvt]].
